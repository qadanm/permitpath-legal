In plain language

  • No account. You never sign up, and you never give us a name or email.
  • No ads, no tracking. No advertising SDK, no third-party analytics SDK, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No developer server. Your answers, scores, bookmarks and progress are stored on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see them.

We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, birthdate, account login, location, contacts, photos, or the advertising identifier.

Your data lives in the app's own container on the device. It may be included in your device backup according to your iOS backup settings. We hold no server-side copy and cannot access it remotely.

Purchases

Full Access is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to load the localized price from Apple, start the purchase, verify the transaction, unlock the features, restore purchases, and handle pending, cancelled, refunded and revoked transactions.

We never receive your full payment card number. Apple processes payment and gives the app a verified entitlement. Apple's own privacy policy and purchase terms apply to that processing.

Deleting the app or your study data does not cancel or refund an Apple purchase. Purchase history and refunds are handled by Apple.

Analytics, diagnostics and tracking

This version contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDK, no cross-app tracking, and no fingerprinting. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.

Apple may collect App Store analytics, crash reports and device diagnostics under Apple's privacy policy. We may see aggregated App Store Connect figures such as downloads and sales. That is Apple's reporting, it is not linked to an account in the app, and it does not come from an SDK inside the app.

If a reviewed analytics vendor or first-party endpoint is ever added, this policy will name the events, the retention period and any consent required before that build ships.

External links

The app can open official California DMV pages in Safari. When you tap one, that site receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser details, under its own privacy policy. We do not attach your study records to the link, and we do not control those sites.

The core study experience is not an embedded web view.

Support and reporting a problem

Support uses the iOS system share sheet. The app prepares a message — question ID, category, source title, content edition, app version, and your description — and you choose where to send it. The app never sends anything automatically and does not create a support account or collect your email address.

If you do email us, we receive what you choose to send, including your email address. Support correspondence is kept for 12 months from the last message in the thread and then deleted; you can ask us to delete it sooner at moe@qadan.co.

Keeping and deleting your data

Local study data stays on your device until you delete it. Settings → Delete local progress removes your answers, sessions, scores, bookmarks and progress. Deleting the app removes its container, subject to iOS backup and restore behaviour.

Because there is no account and no server-side study record, there is no account profile for us to delete. To have support correspondence deleted, email moe@qadan.co.

The app is not available in the first release for now. Narration and other reading features are not enabled, so the app does not send your study content to any speech service.

Children and teenagers

Permit applicants are often teenagers. The app does not ask for a birthdate or an account, and contains no advertising or third-party tracking. It is a study tool for permit applicants. Parents and guardians can use Apple's family purchase controls for the optional in-app purchase.

California privacy rights

We do not collect personal information through an account or a server. If you email support, we hold that correspondence. To exercise a deletion or access request for it, contact moe@qadan.co.

Security

Local study data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and your device's security features, such as a passcode and encryption at rest. We operate no developer server to breach. No method of storage is perfectly secure — keep your device passcode-protected and iOS up to date.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or the law change. The effective date and version at the top will change with it. Material changes to data practices will be described in the app or the release notes.
