Report a problem with a question

The fastest route is inside the app. After checking an answer, tap Report an issue with this question. The app prepares a message containing the question ID, category, source title, content edition and app version, and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where to send it — nothing is sent automatically.

Please include what looks wrong, for example:

  • The correct answer appears to be wrong.
  • The explanation contradicts the answer or the cited rule.
  • The photo does not match what the question describes.
  • A word is misspelled or a sentence is unclear.

App or accessibility problems

If something in the app is broken or hard to use, email moe@qadan.co with:

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The screen you were on and what you expected to happen.
  • Whether a larger text size was active.
  • A screenshot if it helps.

Do not include your name, precise location, or other personal details. We do not need them to fix a bug.

Purchases, refunds and restoring access

Full Access is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. To restore it on a new device or after reinstalling, open PermitPath → Settings → Restore purchases.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com or the purchase history in your Apple Account.

Deleting the app or your study data does not cancel or refund a purchase, and does not revoke Full Access.

Your data

Your study data is stored on your device only — see the privacy policy. To delete it, open PermitPath → Settings → Delete local progress. This removes answers, sessions, scores, bookmarks and progress, and does not affect an Apple purchase.

Where the content comes from

Explanations cite the California Driver's Handbook and official DMV pages. The app does not copy handbook prose and does not claim DMV endorsement. Every question is human-reviewed before it appears, and the approved bank is versioned and fingerprinted so a change is detectable.
